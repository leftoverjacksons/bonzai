Bonzai

A turn-based mobile game about tending a bonsai that will not stay small, climbing through drowned and overgrown ruins. Work in progress.

  • Play: bonsai prototype v2A temple fig that grows by itself through the ruins. Play a hand of three cards (Cut, Pinch, Wire, Rooting powder) in any order; keep it healthy and climbing. Saved in this browser.
  • Rules prototype v0Earlier version, kept for comparison: grow the tree up the shaft with a hand of cards. Progress is saved in this browser.
  • 3D studyThe tree inside a ruined shaft: organic trunk, limited light through openings, a new Atlantis/Angkor/Maya blend each run, and a day/night cycle that advances by turns.
  • Colour studyThree strata (Atlantis, Angkor, Maya) with lighting, atmosphere and glow.
  • Ink studyThe same tree drawn as flat 2D, 2.5D ink wash, and generic 3D.
