How to play

  1. Each turn, play one card (or discard one). Then every growing tip grows one cell.
  2. Growing costs sap. The roots give 1 sap a turn, and each tip in light gives 1 more. Sunlight only reaches tips with open space above them or near a sunlit window.
  3. Night changes the rules. No sunlight at night: only roots, Lanterns and Moonbloom help. Save sap during the day.
  4. Wood costs upkeep. Every 30 cells of wood costs 1 sap a turn, so a taller tree needs more light.
  5. Tips die if stuck for 3 turns or unfed for 4. Long sideways branches sag unless tethered to an iron ring.
  6. Rocks fall into the shaft. A red marker shows where the next one lands.
  7. If no tip can grow, you get one turn of warning, then the run ends. Climb as high as you can.
