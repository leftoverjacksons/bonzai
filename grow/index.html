How to play

Your temple fig grows by itself, toward the light. Left alone it gets leggy, crowded and starved. Your job is the gardener's: you hold three cards and must play all three, in any order; the tree grows after each one. A hand is never three of the same card.

  • Pinch a shoot: it stops, and buds just behind it wake. Denser, more compact foliage, and its energy goes to the rest.
  • Cut a branch: everything beyond is removed and the nearest bud behind the cut wakes. Swipe along the branch to move the cut before you confirm.
  • Wire a shoot to an iron ring on the ruins: it grows toward the ring and will not sag.
  • Rooting powder: wake a dormant bud of your choice on old wood. It grows the way it points.

Shoots bend toward brighter space nearby, and a shoot that meets stone creeps slowly along it toward the light (orange line). The highest shoots take most of the energy. Shoots weaken when they are stuck against stone for more than two turns, sit in shade, go hungry, or run long without branching; long runs sag and can snap. Weak shoots die back to dead wood. Every 8 m is a new stratum: the ruins close in and the fig grows more slowly, since water is harder to lift so high. If no shoots are left, the run ends. Score is height times average health.
