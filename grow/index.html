Growth prototype

This tree grows by itself. Each turn, every part of it reaches toward the light it can find, branches where light comes from more than one side, and thickens as it carries more. Dark chambers slow it down; holes and windows pull it through.

  • Grow advances one turn (two hours) and animates the whole tree growing. Night growth is slower.
  • Drag sideways to walk around the tree. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Drag up or down to look higher or lower; Follow growth brings the camera back.
  • Tap a branch to ride it, then swipe up or down to travel toward its tips or back to the trunk. Tap another branch to jump to it.
