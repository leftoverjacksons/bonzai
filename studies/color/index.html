Three strata, three lights

The same 2.5D renderer as the ink study, now in colour. The sun stays fixed in the world, so as you drag the tree around, its lit side, rim light and shadows change. No 3D engine is involved.

Drag sideways to turn. The sun stays put.

Strata as the climb

These three scenes are proposed as layers of one run, bottom to top. You start in a flooded Atlantean court, climb through a jungle-swallowed Angkor temple, and break above the clouds onto Maya sky steps. Each boundary is a natural place for an unlock and for a tree to qualify for the gallery.

What the renderer does

  • Lighting from 3D. Every branch, leaf clump and pillar keeps a 3D position and normal. Brightness comes from the angle to the sun, then is painted as a 2D stroke.
  • Rim light and translucency. When the sun is behind the tree, edges glow and leaves turn yellow-green, as backlit leaves do.
  • Atmosphere. Distant strokes fade toward the sky's colour. Mist, water or clouds hide the base as you climb.
  • Additive light. Sun bloom, light shafts, glowing blossoms and particles add light rather than paint over.
  • Cost. The counter at the bottom left shows frames per second. It is Canvas 2D only, which suits phones.
